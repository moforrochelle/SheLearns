Board Rules

Welcome to the SheLearns board. This is a place for girls to help each other learn. Please follow these rules so everyone feels safe.

  1. Be kind. No insults, teasing, or making fun of anyone's questions or answers. Every girl here is still learning.
  2. Keep your private information private. Do not share your real name, phone number, address, school, or social media. Use only your username.
  3. Do not ask others for their private information. If someone asks for yours, do not answer, and report the post.
  4. Stay on topic. Post about studying, school subjects, study tips, or finding a study partner.
  5. Share only advice you trust. If you are not sure an answer is correct, say so. Never give advice about health, medicine, or anything dangerous. Ask a teacher or a trusted adult instead.
  6. Do not post links to other websites or groups unless the moderator has approved them.
  7. Report anything that worries you. Use the Report button if a post is unkind, unsafe, or makes you uncomfortable. You will not get in trouble for reporting.

How it works: Every post is checked by a moderator before it appears. Some posts may be blocked automatically if they contain things like phone numbers. If your post is not approved, you can change it and try again.

If you ever feel unsafe or someone makes you uncomfortable, talk to a trusted adult, such as a parent, guardian, or teacher.
